== Why the EX hold is needed (holdexp.S on the no_hold mutant, simulation probe)
A vector load/store resets vstart on its last MEM cycle, the same edge on which EX advances, so
an instruction in EX right behind it is judged on the old vstart. The first version of
vstart_illegal_directed (load/store right after csrw vstart) passed on the no_hold mutant: a
$display probe showed EX empty (idex_valid 0) during every resumed load, because the fetch had
not caught up. holdexp.S tries several sequences in front of `csrw vstart, 3; vle8.v; vredsum.vs`;
with a scalar `ld` just before the vector load, the reduction sat in EX for 129 cycles while the
load was in MEM, and the no_hold mutant took 3 illegal-instruction traps (core_tb default config;
none with --no-cache). vstart_illegal_directed cases 31-34 now run that shape (a scalar load
first) three times, and case 31 fails on the mutant as listed above.
